# AC-CCM

AC-CCM is a dataset for aesthetic mood recognition in Chinese traditional music. Based on the theory of the Twenty-Four Conditions in the Qing-dynasty qin treatise Xishan Qinkuang, it was constructed for tasks involving emotion, aesthetic mood, and aesthetic semantic understanding in Chinese music. It can be used for music aesthetic mood recognition, audio classification, multi-label annotation prediction, and research on relationships between traditional musical aesthetic concepts and audio features.

The dataset centers on the Twenty-Four Conditions proposed in Xishan Qinkuang to describe aesthetic mood, character, and aesthetic features in Chinese traditional music. Unlike common emotion classification data, AC-CCM focuses more on aesthetic semantic expression in the context of Chinese traditional music, providing experimental resources for music information retrieval, computational musicology, and intelligent understanding of Chinese traditional music.

This section may later be expanded with dataset size, instrument composition, audio format, annotation granularity, field definitions, and quality control procedures. Fields that can currently be described include piece or audio excerpt identifiers, audio paths or relative indices, aesthetic mood labels, instrument information, and metadata such as piece, version, and performer.
